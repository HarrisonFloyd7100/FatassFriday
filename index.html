SIMPLE RULES. ENDLESS POSSIBILITIES.

Play. Climb. Repeat.

01

Win your card

Each card holds two players. An organizer selects the winner. That adds one win and one loss immediately, and saves the match to history.

02

Finish the round

After every paired card has a result or is skipped, advance together. Winners move one card up; losers move one down. The top winner and bottom loser stay.

03

Keep it going

A solo player gets a bye and moves up one card next round, unless a skipped card blocks it, without a win or loss. A player alone at the top stays there. Nobody is automatically eliminated.

+

Make room

New players join the bottom. An open solo card fills first; otherwise a new card opens. Existing player positions and recorded results stay intact.

↺

Correct a selection

Before advancing, choose the other winner on that card. Records and the same history entry are corrected, without counting a second match. Use Undo result to clear a mistaken result. Completed rounds can be reopened with Manage round → Roll back one round, if a checkpoint is available.

W/L

Count an extra match

Pick any two players and record who won. Their records update and the match appears in history. Their cards do not change.

Ⅱ

Sit this one out

Skip a card when its players cannot meet. They stay in place with no score; their card becomes a boundary. Players above and below move within their own groups. Skips reset next round.

✎

Fix the pairing

Edit a card to swap active players. Changed cards have this round’s results undone. Delete a mistaken card to take its players off the ladder; earlier records and past matches remain available.

↶

Manage the round

Organizers can change the displayed round number, clear this round’s card results, or roll back to the previous saved round. Rollback discards changes since that round advanced. Checkpoints begin with advances made after installing round management.

≡

Correct match history

Use a history entry’s ⋯ menu to edit its winner, delete it, or ignore it for win/loss totals. Ignored entries stay visible and can be restored. Historical corrections do not rearrange the active ladder, and saved rollback checkpoints are updated too.
